6. Key Qualitative Feedback

27MY System - Positive Themes

  • Smoother steering intervention and cornering performance
  • More natural braking behavior
  • Increased driver confidence in the system
  • Improved GUI with clear vehicle/environment recognition
  • Touch-sensitive steering wheel (vs. torque-based) greatly preferred
  • Lane changes feel seamless and natural
  • System available on non-interstate highways now

27MY System - Concerns

  • Off-highway use: acceleration logic during lane changes is concerning off-highway
  • Complexity: too many indicators and conditions for activation
  • Turn signal interaction model (half vs. full engagement) is confusing
  • Cost/marketability concerns for average Nissan customer

Multi-Lane Dynamic Graphics (Avg: 4.29/5)

  • Positive: Informative without being distracting; good balance
  • Positive: Seeing recognized vehicles/objects builds confidence
  • Concern: ICC distance indicator too small (prefer previous larger bars)
  • Suggestion: Add arrow/gradient for lane change path directionality

Lane Change Assist (Avg: 4.14/5)

  • Positive: Smooth, predictable, "wow factor" for some respondents
  • Positive: Easy toggle via turn signal once understood
  • Concern: Must wait for arrow indicators before activating signal
  • Concern: Half-engagement vs full-engagement confusion
  • Recommendation: Customer tutorial needed during sales process

Hands-Off Traffic Jam Support (Avg: 3.57/5)

  • Concern: 30-31 mph limit too low for US traffic jams (frequently 0-40 mph)
  • Concern: Constant hand-on/off cycling would frustrate customers
  • Concern: Limited value if still need to be ready to take over
  • Concern: Highway-only limitation reduces use cases
  • Positive: System performed well within its operating parameters

7. Recommendations

  1. Raise Hands-Off speed threshold for US market — Multiple respondents indicated 31 mph is too low. Real US traffic jams often flow at 30-40 mph.
  2. Simplify activation UI/indicators — Several respondents found the number of indicators and conditions confusing. Streamline the interface.
  3. Clarify turn signal interaction model — Half-engagement vs full-engagement behavior was confusing. Consider standardizing or adding clear cues.
  4. Develop customer tutorial/onboarding — Build a tutorial into sales/delivery and consider an in-vehicle guided walkthrough.
  5. Address off-highway behavior — Acceleration logic during lane changes carries over to off-highway scenarios (e.g., left-turn lanes).
